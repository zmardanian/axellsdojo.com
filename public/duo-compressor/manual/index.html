Axell Duo Compressor: getting started and every control

Two classic compressors and a true-peak limiter, in one. This page explains every control, with the plugin's own screens. Back to Axell Duo Compressor.

Getting started

  1. Install. Windows: run the Axell Signature Suite installer (or copy Axell Duo Compressor.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3). Linux: unpack the archive and run ./install.sh.
  2. Rescan. Let your DAW rescan its VST3 plugins; Axell Duo Compressor appears under the vendor Axell Yobag.
  3. Insert it on the track or bus it is made for, and start from a factory preset close to your material.
  4. Listen, then adjust the few controls described below; A/B compares two settings without losing either.

What it does

A FET peak compressor, an opto leveling amplifier and a true-peak limiter in one plugin, built for rap and boom-bap vocals and just as much at home on bass, drums, mix bus and master. Formerly Duo Vocal Comp: projects saved with Duo Vocal Comp 1.0 open in Axell Duo Compressor with every setting and render bit-identically (checked through a VST3 host, difference exactly 0).

FET (modelled on the classic 1176-type peak limiter) catches peaks: INPUT drives into the threshold, 20-800 µs attack, 50-1100 ms program-dependent release, ratios 4 / 8 / 12 / 20 / ALL ("all buttons in").

  • Detector: VINTAGE or CLEAN. Vintage behaves like the hardware: fast settings add some low-end distortion on bass and 808s. Clean keeps the same attack and curve with a ripple-free detector: THD+N -142 dB at 30 and 100 Hz with the fastest settings (Vintage: -43 / -46 dB).
  • Threshold, knee (Auto = the classic ratio-dependent knee), range (maximum gain reduction) and hold.

OPTO (modelled on the LA-2A-type leveling amplifier) levels the performance: a fast grab, a two-stage program-dependent release, Compress / Limit, HF emphasis, plus threshold, knee, range and hold.

  • Opto Model: MEASURED or CLASSIC. Measured (the default) is calibrated on recordings of a real LA-2A: the same Peak Reduction setting compresses like the hardware's (see Validation). Classic is the Duo Vocal Comp 1.x law; projects from 1.x load with it and sound as before.

LIMITER: a true-peak brickwall at the end of the chain. Ceiling -24 to 0 dBTP, release, lookahead 0.5-5 ms. 8x true-peak detection. No sample peak ever passes the ceiling, and no inter-sample (true) peak does for content up to 0.48 fs (21 kHz at 44.1 kHz) - see the limiter's limits.

SIDE-CHAIN: HPF, LPF and a bell EQ for both detectors (they never change your sound), an external side-chain input (ducking, keyed compression: KEY = EXT per stage) and SC LISTEN.

GLOBAL: stage order, oversampling 1x / 2x / 4x (default: aliasing below -149 dBFS even driven hard), Stereo or Mid/Side, compressor lookahead 0-10 ms (catches transients before they arrive: 23 dB of onset overshoot down to 1.3 dB with 2 ms), Color (analog-style harmonics), stereo link, parallel Mix (sample-aligned), output, auto makeup.

VOICE HOLD (3.0): in the pauses of your voice the compressor holds its gain at your voice's level instead of releasing, so the room, breaths and headphone bleed between phrases are not pulled up (real take: pauses +23.4 dB against the voice without it, +5.9 dB with it, your voice compressed exactly the same). It uses Axell Gate's voice detector, adds no latency, and is on for new instances and the vocal presets. Turn it off on drums, beats and buses (their presets do): the detector is trained on voices and takes drum hits for one.

ESS HOLD (4.0): through each ess of your voice the compressor holds the gain reduction of the word it belongs to instead of releasing, so the S, SH and CH sounds are not pushed above your vowels; after the ess it glides back (10 ms) to exactly what it would otherwise do. It uses Axell Velvet's sibilance detector (zero latency) and is on for new instances and the vocal presets; off on beats and buses (the detector fires on hi-hats now and then).

DISPLAY: a 5-second history of input and output level with each stage's gain reduction, a transfer curve of the whole chain at the current settings, and COMPARE: hear your unprocessed track at exactly the processed track's loudness, so you judge what the compression does and not the level change.

Presets for vocals, mix bus, mastering, bass and side-chain ducking; A/B compare; user presets in ~/.local/share/AxellDuoCompressor/Presets (Duo Vocal Comp presets are listed too). Latency is reported to the DAW and changes only after the DAW has been told (no misaligned audio when you switch lookahead, limiter or oversampling).

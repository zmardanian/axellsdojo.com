Two legends and a limiter, in one plugin. A FET peak compressor, an opto leveling amplifier calibrated on recordings of real hardware, and a true-peak limiter - built for rap and boom-bap vocals, and just as much at home on bass, drums, the bus and the master.

01   WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Five reasons, all measured.

An opto that follows the hardware

calibrated on LA-2A recordings, it tracks the real unit's gain reduction to 0.6 dB RMS on music it never saw.

ESS HOLD

compressors lift esses above the vowels; Duo holds the word's gain through each ess: +3.28 -> +0.60 dB on 28 real takes.

VOICE HOLD

the room stays down between your phrases: pauses +23.4 -> +5.9 dB on a real take, the voice's compression unchanged.

Clean where it should be

aliasing -149.5 dBFS at 4x on a brutal test; silence in, exactly silence out.

Your old sessions are safe

projects from Duo Vocal Comp 1.0 render bit-identically.

Every figure comes from the plugin's own test suites and published benchmarks — the full measurements, the method and every version's history are on GitHub.

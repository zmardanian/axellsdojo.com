01   WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Five reasons, all measured.

An opto that follows the hardware

Calibrated on LA-2A recordings, it tracks the real unit's gain reduction within 0.6 dB on music it never saw.

ESS HOLD

Compressors push esses above the vowels. Duo holds the word's gain through each ess: +3.28 dB down to +0.60 dB on 28 real takes.

VOICE HOLD

The room stays down between your phrases (pauses +23.4 dB down to +5.9 dB on a real take), while the voice is compressed as before.

Clean where it should be

Aliasing stays at -149.5 dBFS at 4x on a brutal test - and silence in gives exactly silence out.

Your old sessions are safe

Projects from Duo Vocal Comp 1.0 render bit-identically.

Every figure comes from the plugin's own test suites and published benchmarks — the full measurements, the method and every version's history are on GitHub.

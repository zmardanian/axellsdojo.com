CREDITS

The music in the examples.

Every before/after example on this site is made from the OnAir Music Dataset - stems of royalty-free songs by the OnAir Music project (Hrag, the Crescent MTL Project and SevagH), featuring KXNE and Holocene, released under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence.

Tracks used: Ronin (feat. KXNE) - verse, drums, instrumental, full mix; Set Me Free - vocal; Under My Skin - piano; Bouncin Around - drums; Autumn in NY - keys.

Changes made: 14-second excerpts, set to a working level (-18 LUFS; -20 LUFS for the mastering examples), processed through the Axell plugins with their factory presets, the processed version matched to the original's loudness (except the mastering examples), short fades. Where marked, a small room (De-Reverb) or headphone bleed of the song's instrumental (Gate) was simulated before processing. The processed excerpts are shared under the same licence, CC BY-SA 4.0. No endorsement by the artists or the OnAir Music project is implied.

Typefaces: Great Vibes, Cormorant Garamond, Jost and Yuji Syuku (SIL Open Font License), served from this site - no request goes to a font service.

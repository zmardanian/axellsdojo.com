Privacy policy

What happens to your data when you visit axellsdojo.com - in short: no accounts, no advertising, no tracking cookies, and no IP address is ever stored by this site. As of 20 November 2026.

2. Hosting and delivery (Cloudflare)

The site is served by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, which processes data on my behalf (data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR). To deliver pages and protect the site against attacks, Cloudflare processes your IP address, the time, the page requested, your browser's user agent and the referring page in its server logs, briefly and under its own retention rules. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR); its data processing agreement also contains the EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR) as a further safeguard. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR - the legitimate interest in a secure, working website.

Cloudflare's bot protection may set a technically necessary cookie (__cf_bm, valid 30 minutes) that tells people from automated attacks; it is required for the service you asked for (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG) and not used for anything else.

3. Visitor statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

The pages load Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views and load times without cookies, without storing IP addresses and without following you across sites; I only see totals. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR - the legitimate interest in knowing which pages are used.

4. The download counter

The download buttons go through axellsdojo.com/get/... before they forward you to the file on GitHub. Each download stores: the time, the plugin, Windows or Linux, the version, the country and city and the name of your internet provider (derived by Cloudflare from your IP address), the page you came from, your device type (e.g. Windows, iOS) and browser family. Your IP address itself is not stored. It is used only for a moment to limit downloads from one address to ten a minute, so the counter cannot be flooded. The records are kept in Cloudflare's D1 database and deleted automatically after 24 months. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR - the legitimate interest in knowing how often and where the plugins are used.

If you open axellsdojo.com/get/not-me, a cookie (axell_owner, 10 years) marks your browser's downloads as the author's own tests. It is only ever set when you open that address yourself.

5. Downloads, Ko-fi and other links

Plugin files are downloaded from GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA); tips are handled by Ko-fi (Ko-fi Labs Limited, UK) and its payment providers. When you follow such a link, the respective provider processes your data under its own privacy policy. This site embeds nothing from them - no scripts, no frames, no tracking pixels. Fonts, images and audio are served from this site only; no request goes to a font or media service.

6. Email and the contact form

If you write to contact@axellsdojo.com, the message is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to my mailbox and kept as long as needed to answer it and for any follow-up. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) or (f) GDPR.

The contact form sends your name (optional), your email address and your message the same way: as an email through Cloudflare's email service to my mailbox, where it is kept as long as needed to answer it. The website itself keeps no copy; it notes only the time of each sent message (no content, no address, no IP address) to stop mass sending, and deletes that note after 30 days. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) or (f) GDPR.

To keep automated spam out, the contact page loads Cloudflare Turnstile (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). It checks in the background that a person, not a program, is sending the form; to do so your browser exchanges technical signals with Cloudflare (such as the browser and device type and your IP address). Turnstile sets no advertising cookies and does not track you across sites; Cloudflare states that it uses this data only to provide the check. Only the page with the form loads it. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR - the legitimate interest in a contact form free of spam. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. If you prefer, write to contact@axellsdojo.com instead.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR). Write to contact@axellsdojo.com. You may also complain to a data protection authority - for me, Der Hessische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Wiesbaden (datenschutz.hessen.de). There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

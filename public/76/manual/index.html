Axell 76: getting started and every control

The FET limiting amplifier, modelled from the real thing. This page explains every control, with the plugin's own screens. Back to Axell 76.

Getting started

  1. Install. Windows: run the Axell Signature Suite installer (or copy Axell 76.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3). Linux: unpack the archive and run ./install.sh.
  2. Rescan. Let your DAW rescan its VST3 plugins; Axell 76 appears under the vendor Axell Yobag.
  3. Insert it on the track or bus it is made for, and start from a factory preset close to your material.
  4. Listen, then adjust the few controls described below; A/B compares two settings without losing either.

The instrument

The 1176 workflowINPUT drives into a fixed threshold, OUTPUT sets the level. ATTACK and RELEASE 1 - 7 (7 fastest), with the hardware's program-dependent release. RATIO 4 · 8 · 12 · 20 · ALL - each with its own threshold and slope, and ALL's famous over-compression.
FET characterEven harmonics that grow with gain reduction, odd-harmonic bite, the grit of a fast release on bass, a delicate low thump on every clamp, the coupling low-cut, the FET's soft limit.
Modern controlMIX for parallel compression (delay-matched), latency-compensated IN / OUT, QUALITY 2x · 4x · 8x oversampling, side-chain high-pass, external side-chain input.
The meterGain reduction, or a classic VU of the output at +4 or +8 (true VU ballistics: 300 ms, as a hardware meter) - click the meter to switch.
The cabinetFour walnut boards with mitred corners and a hairline seam, brushed aluminium, an aged amber-lit VU, in SILVER or BLACK; textures render at your screen's real pixel density.
Safe sessionsProjects keep the sound they were made with: 2.0, 3.0/3.1 and 3.2 sessions each render bit-for-bit as on their own release.
RobustAny block size, mono or stereo, 44.1 - 192 kHz; NaN- and denormal-safe; click-free automation; state round-trips exactly.

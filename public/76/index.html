01   WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Five reasons, all measured.

As close as the reference emulation

0.44 dB from a real 1176LN on notes it never trained on - level with the UADx 1176 at its best-matching attack setting.

A waveform you can trust

median error against the real 1176LN's own output: -25.5 dB.

Clean when you slam it

aliasing -68 to -90 dB on hard, fast settings; the UADx measures -40 to -56 dB.

All five ratios

4, 8, 12, 20 and the all-buttons-in mode, with the FET's own distortion and timing.

Light

about 2 % of one core at 4x oversampling, always compressing.

Every figure comes from the plugin's own test suites and published benchmarks — the full measurements, the method and every version's history are on GitHub.

Recorded in a closet, a bedroom or a car? Axell De-Reverb takes the room out. A trained network removes the tail and the 350-600 Hz box of small rooms - the part most de-reverbs leave alone because they were built for halls - while TRUE TONE keeps your voice's real tone.

01   WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Five reasons, all measured.

Better than the research standard

On voices and rooms it never heard, quality (ViSQOL) rises from 3.96 to 4.13 - the research standard reaches 3.99.

Built for small rooms

Booths, closets, cars and bathrooms: it removes the box and the early reflections, not just the tail.

TRUE TONE

A learned tone correction gives back the low end that room removal takes away.

Record through it

LIVE mode while you track, STUDIO mode with look-ahead when you mix.

Stereo stays stereo

Both channels share one filter, so a stereo vocal keeps its image: 2.55 dB of drift against the true signal down to 0.72 dB.

Every figure comes from the plugin's own test suites and published benchmarks — the full measurements, the method and every version's history are on GitHub.

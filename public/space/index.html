01   WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Five reasons, all measured.

Eight REAL SPACES

Vocal booth, tiled bathroom, living room, live room, stairwell, small club, wood chapel and car park - each held to its physics.

Tails without hiss

ESS DUCK keeps the esses 28 dB under the vowels in the tail - the clearest of every reverb measured.

Decay you can trust

The measured decay lands within 3 % of the knob, turns dense within 15 ms and never rings metallic.

Effects that step aside

UNMASK ducks only the frequency bands your voice is using at that moment.

Zero latency

Use it live, on a send or right on the track.

Every figure comes from the plugin's own test suites and published benchmarks — the full measurements, the method and every version's history are on GitHub.

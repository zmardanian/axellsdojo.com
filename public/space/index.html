Reverb and delay that make room for the voice. A 32-line true-stereo reverb, eight real rooms modelled from their size and walls, and a tempo-synced delay - with ESS DUCK keeping the esses out of the tails and UNMASK stepping the effects back only where they cover your words.

01   WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Five reasons, all measured.

Eight REAL SPACES

vocal booth, tiled bathroom, living room, live room, stairwell, small club, wood chapel, car park - held to their physics.

Tails without hiss

ESS DUCK leaves the esses 28 dB under the vowels in the tail - the clearest of every reverb measured.

Decay you can trust

the measured decay time within 3 % of the knob, dense within 15 ms, no metallic ringing.

Effects that step aside

UNMASK ducks only the bands your voice is using at that moment.

Zero latency

use it live, on a send or on the track.

Every figure comes from the plugin's own test suites and published benchmarks — the full measurements, the method and every version's history are on GitHub.

Loud masters that stay clean - even after Spotify and YouTube encode them. A 16x oversampled clipper into a true-peak limiter, a broadcast-standard loudness meter, and a live check of your master through the real streaming codecs, with SAFE to keep it from clipping after the encode.

01   WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Five reasons, all measured.

Streaming-safe, proven

after SAFE: 0 clipped samples through Opus, Vorbis, AAC and MP3 on 9 loud rap masters.

A ceiling it actually holds

worst -1.017 dBTP at a -1 dBTP ceiling on mixes driven +12 to +14 dB, measured by an independent reconstruction.

Clean bass under heavy limiting

40-50 Hz at 6 dB of limiting: -139 dB THD+N (Transparent).

Made for vocals too

VOICE HOLD and ESS HOLD keep pauses and esses where they belong on a vocal bus.

16 % lighter in 4.0.3

22 % on Windows - byte-for-byte the same sound.

Every figure comes from the plugin's own test suites and published benchmarks — the full measurements, the method and every version's history are on GitHub.

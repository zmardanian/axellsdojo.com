03   THE DOJO

Your chain, tuned to your voice.

Record or drop one dry take. The Dojo measures your voice and your room, tunes a complete vocal chain by rendering your take through the plugins, and saves it as presets inside the plugins — so your signature sound opens in whatever DAW you use, on Windows or Linux.

  • Installs, updates and restores every plugin — checksum-verified, for every DAW
  • Voice Profile: loudness, noise, pitch, esses, room and low end — tuned presets for Gate, Penta EQ, Duo Compressor and Velvet
  • Streaming Preview: hear your master as Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube will play it
  • REAPER extras: one-click sessions and twelve ready-made FX chains

04   GET IT

First-class on Windows. Native on Linux.

One installer

The Axell Signature Suite installer puts the Dojo and all nine plugins on Windows 10 / 11 in one step — each one optional.

Tested on every build

Every Windows build is made and tested by GitHub Actions and checked to sound the same as the Linux build — most of them bit for bit. None of them allocates memory while it processes audio (pluginval 2.0, strictest level).

Free, for good

Open source under the AGPLv3. No accounts, no licence keys, no subscriptions, no telemetry — the Dojo only asks GitHub for new versions.

03   THE DOJO

Your chain, tuned to your voice.

Record or drop one dry take. The Dojo measures your voice and your room, tunes a complete vocal chain by rendering your take through the plugins, and opens it in REAPER — your signature sound, one click away.

  • Installs, updates and restores every plugin — checksum-verified
  • Voice Profile: loudness, noise, pitch, esses, room and low end
  • Streaming Preview: hear your master as Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube will play it
  • Twelve ready-made REAPER chains

01   WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Five reasons, all measured.

Analog-matched to 20 kHz

Every curve lands within 0.003 dB (Zero Latency), 0.05 dB (Natural) and 0.10 dB (Linear Phase) of the analog response.

An EQ band that knows what an ess is

Any band can follow the suite's sibilance network: the esses are cut as hard as a self-keyed band would, the vowels stay untouched.

Dynamic and spectral bands

With Mid/Side, side-chain and three phase modes, from zero latency to linear phase.

Character on tap

Inductor, Console E/G, Passive Tube and Air: four colour modules in one rack.

Audited, not assumed

1,526 random settings were checked against the analog curves by an independent host - none exempt.

Every figure comes from the plugin's own test suites and published benchmarks — the full measurements, the method and every version's history are on GitHub.

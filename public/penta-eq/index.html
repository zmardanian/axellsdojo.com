01   WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Five reasons, all measured.

Analog-matched to 20 kHz

Clean curves within 0.003 dB (Zero Latency), 0.05 dB (Natural) and 0.10 dB (Linear Phase) of the analog response.

An EQ band that knows what an ess is

any band can follow the suite's sibilance network: esses cut as hard as a self-keyed band, vowels untouched.

Dynamic and spectral bands

with Mid/Side, side-chain, and three phase modes from zero latency to linear phase.

Character on tap

Inductor, Console E/G, Passive Tube and Air - four modules in a rack.

Audited, not assumed

1526 random settings checked against scipy's analog curves by an independent host - none exempt.

Every figure comes from the plugin's own test suites and published benchmarks — the full measurements, the method and every version's history are on GitHub.

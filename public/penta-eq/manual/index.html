MANUAL · ANALOG-MATCHED EQ

Axell Penta EQ: getting started and every control

The analog-matched EQ that listens to your esses. This page explains every control, with the plugin's own screens. Back to Axell Penta EQ.

Getting started

  1. Install. Windows: run the Axell Signature Suite installer (or copy Axell Penta EQ.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3). Linux: unpack the archive and run ./install.sh.
  2. Rescan. Let your DAW rescan its VST3 plugins; Axell Penta EQ appears under the vendor Axell Yobag.
  3. Insert it on the track or bus it is made for, and start from a factory preset close to your material.
  4. Listen, then adjust the few controls described below; A/B compares two settings without losing either.

What it does

An all-round equalizer VST3 for Linux and Windows by Axell Yobag: a surgical, analog-matched parametric EQ with dynamic and spectral bands, Mid/Side, curve drawing and reference matching, plus a rack of four character modules - Inductor, Console E/G, Passive Tube and Air. Built for rap and boom bap vocals, beats, 808s, the mix bus and the master.

Parametric core

  • 12 bands + dedicated high-pass and low-pass. Types: bell, low/high shelf, low/high cut (6–96 dB/oct with resonance), notch, band-pass, tilt.
  • Clean mode: curves match the analog prototypes within ±0.1 dB up to 20 kHz - no "cramping" of high bells and shelves near the top of the spectrum, for every band type, gain, Q and frequency at 44.1–192 kHz, without exceptions (measured independently; see Accuracy).
  • Character mode per band: proportional Q (wider at small gains, narrower at big ones), cuts tighter than boosts, and boosts that saturate gently with Drive.
  • Dynamic per band: threshold, range (cut or boost when loud), ratio, attack, release. The detector can listen to the band's own region, to another band's region (cut 3 kHz when 9 kHz gets loud) or to the **external side-chain (duck the 808's fundamental with the kick), or (3.0) to the esses**: the band moves only when Axell Velvet's sibilance network hears an S, SH, Z or CH, so a de-essing band leaves bright vowels and consonants alone (a quarter of the collateral of a self-keyed band, measured on held-out voices). De-essing, harshness, mud and boom control.
  • Spectral dynamics per band: instead of moving as a whole, every frequency inside the band reacts on its own - only what is too loud gets cut (a resonance, a whistle, a harsh partial, a sharp sibilant), the rest of the band stays untouched. Zero added latency; the graph shows the live per-frequency gain.
  • Per band placement: Stereo / Left / Right / Mid / Side.
  • Solo a band's region to find problem frequencies.

Character rack (each switchable, before or after the parametric EQ)

  • Inductor - class-A inductor channel EQ: 18 dB/oct HPF (50/80/160/300 Hz), resonant low shelf (35/60/110/220 Hz), musical mid (360 Hz–7.2 kHz), very gentle 12 kHz shelf, transformer saturation that grabs the lows first.
  • Console E/G - four-band console channel EQ with HPF/LPF and bell switches. E: constant bandwidth, punchy; G: proportional Q and shelves with a little overshoot. Console clipping with Drive.
  • Passive Tube - passive LC program EQ with tube makeup. Boost and attenuate the same low frequency for the classic trick: weight at the bottom and a dip just above that clears the mud. Broad high boost with bandwidth, separate high attenuation shelf, push-pull tube colour.
  • Air - an ultra-wide, boost-only high shelf (2.5–40 kHz) that opens the top without extra sibilance.

Global

  • Phase modes: Zero Latency (analog magnitude, no delay - for tracking), Natural Phase (analog magnitude and phase, 2.7 ms), Linear Phase (no phase shift; Low / Medium / High resolution). Latency is always reported to the host.
  • Oversampling 1x / 2x / 4x for the drive stages, global Drive, Input/Output gain, Mix, static loudness-matched Auto Gain (never pumps).
  • Spectrum analyzer: input (pre), output (post) and side-chain spectra, 1/6-octave smoothing, 4.5 dB/oct tilt, peak hold, adjustable speed and EQ scale.
  • Workflow: select several bands and move them together, keyboard editing, copy/paste (also between instances), invert, note readout and snapping, a band on a spectrum peak with one click, Draw a curve and let the EQ build it, Match your track to a reference, Gain Scale (0–200 %).
  • 42 factory presets (all loudness-matched), user presets with descriptions, preset browser with categories and search, A/B compare (saved with the session), undo/redo.
  • Resizable vector GUI (75–200 %), sharp on HiDPI, tooltips on every control.

The readout shows the note of the frequency (e.g. A4 +20ct); Snap frequency to notes (analyzer or graph menu) makes drags and nudges land on semitones. SCALE in the graph's top bar scales the gain of every band (0–200 %, double-click = 100 %).

DRAW: paint the curve you want across the graph (several strokes are fine; unpainted parts count as flat), then APPLY - the EQ fits up to 12 bands to it (one undo step). MATCH: play a reference (a mix you like) through the side-chain, through this instance, or through another instance of the EQ in the session and capture it; play your track and capture it; set amount, smoothing and the largest correction and press APPLY. The level difference is removed - it matches tone, not loudness.

Dynamic bands show a bar from their static gain to the end of their range; the white curve moves live, the faint curve is the static setting. The ANALYZER menu in the graph's corner sets pre/post/side-chain display, peak hold, tilt, speed and the EQ scale (±6/12/18/30 dB).

### Band panel Band chips HP 1 … 12 LP select a band (grey chips are free: clicking one switches it on as a flat bell). SOLO (hold) plays only the band's region, BYPASS keeps the band but disables it, REMOVE deletes it. The right half holds the DYNAMIC section: threshold, range, ratio, attack, release, the detector source (Self, Band n, External or Ess) and BROAD / SPECTRAL. Spectral thresholds are per frequency: loud music reads about −45 to −25 dBFS per frequency, so around −30 dB the band catches ringing peaks and lets the rest pass.

### Header Preset menu (opens the browser), previous/next, SAVE, A / B / COPY, UNDO / REDO (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y), phase mode, linear-phase resolution, oversampling, interface size.

### Side-chain in REAPER The plugin has a stereo side-chain input (plug-in pins 3/4). On the track with Axell Penta EQ, set the track to 4 channels; on the trigger track (e.g. the kick) add a send to that track, Audio: 1/2 → 3/4. Then set a dynamic band's detector to External. The presets Bass/Kick Carve and Sample Chop - Room for Vocals use this.

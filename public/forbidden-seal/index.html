01   WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Five reasons, all measured.

Decoupled saturation

Your highs stay silky and your mids never turn boxy - the voice itself never passes the circuit.

Measured against a respected tape emulation

At equal squash: less boxiness at 61 of 64 points, with the predicted listener score matched at every point.

Eight circuits from physics

Tape hysteresis, triode, push-pull pentode, console, germanium, iron, diode and fold.

Esses out of the grit

ESS GUARD keeps the esses clean: guarded esses carry -137 dB of residue.

A face like no other

DRIVE is a katana drawn from its scabbard - and the blade glows hotter as you push it.

Every figure comes from the plugin's own test suites and published benchmarks — the full measurements, the method and every version's history are on GitHub.

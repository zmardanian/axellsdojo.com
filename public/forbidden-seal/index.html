01   WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Five reasons, all measured.

Decoupled saturation

your highs stay silky, your mids never turn to a box - the voice itself is never processed.

Measured against a respected tape emulation

at equal squash: less box at 61 of 64 points, the predicted listener score matched at every point.

Eight circuits from physics

tape hysteresis, triode, push-pull pentode, console, germanium, iron, diode, fold.

Esses out of the grit

ESS GUARD keeps them clean: guarded esses at -137 dB of residue.

A face like no other

DRIVE is a katana drawn from its scabbard; the blade heats with the drive.

Every figure comes from the plugin's own test suites and published benchmarks — the full measurements, the method and every version's history are on GitHub.

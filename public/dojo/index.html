Your whole vocal chain, tuned to your voice from one dry take. The home of the Axell Signature suite: it installs and updates every plugin, measures your voice and your room, tunes a complete chain by rendering your take through the plugins and saves it as presets inside them - so it opens in any DAW, on Windows and Linux.

01   WHAT IT DOES

Everything around the plugins.

Any DAW, Windows and Linux

the plugins and the presets the Dojo tunes for you load in every DAW that hosts VST3 - FL Studio, Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One, Bitwig, REAPER, Ardour.

One installer on Windows

the Dojo and all nine plugins, each one optional.

A chain tuned to you

Voice Profile measures loudness, noise, pitch, your esses, the room and the low end, then tunes the chain on your take.

Your signature sound, saved

your approved vocal and master settings as presets - plus, for REAPER users, a one-click session from a dry take.

Safe updates

every archive checked against its published SHA-256; the previous version kept as a backup.

Hear it like your listeners

Streaming Preview: what Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Tidal and Amazon Music do to your master.

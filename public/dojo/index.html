Windows says “Windows protected your PC”?

The installer is free and not yet code-signed, so SmartScreen doesn't know it. Click More info, then Run anyway. To be sure it's the original file, compare its fingerprint with SHA256SUMS.txt on the release page — in PowerShell: Get-FileHash .\AxellSignatureSuite-*-setup.exe. On Linux: sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt. Every file is built from the public source on GitHub, and the checksums are signed — see the code signing policy.

Your whole vocal chain, tuned to your voice from one dry take. The home of the Axell Signature suite: it installs and updates every plugin, measures your voice and your room, tunes a complete chain by rendering your take through the plugins and saves it as presets inside them - so it opens in any DAW, on Windows and Linux.

01   WHAT IT DOES

Everything around the plugins.

Any DAW, Windows and Linux

The plugins and the presets the Dojo tunes for you load in every VST3 host - FL Studio, Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One, Bitwig, REAPER, Ardour.

One installer on Windows

The Dojo and all nine plugins in one step, each one optional.

A chain tuned to you

Voice Profile measures loudness, noise, pitch, your esses, the room and the low end - then tunes the chain on your own take.

Your signature sound, saved

Your approved vocal and master settings become presets - plus, in REAPER, a one-click session from a dry take.

Safe updates

Every download is checked against its signed checksum, and the previous version is kept as a backup.

Hear it like your listeners

Streaming Preview plays what Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Tidal and Amazon Music do to your master.

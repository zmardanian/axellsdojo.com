A de-esser that recognises an S instead of listening for loud treble. Trained on 111 voices, it cuts the harsh esses and leaves everything else alone - and three analog warmth models give the voice its glow, with S-GUARD keeping the warmth off the esses.

01   WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Five reasons, all measured.

30-80x gentler on your vowels

than a respected sibilance-aware de-esser, at equal harsh-ess removal, on 15 voices it never trained on.

Trained on 111 voices

and it hears accents it has never met: F1 0.82 on 12 unseen accents.

Zero latency when you record

the LIVE de-esser is causal - with HEAT off, Velvet adds no latency at all.

Warmth that stays clean

aliasing at equal saturation: tape -108, console -129, tube -113 dBc - certified UADx plugins measured -70 to -96.

3-9 % lighter in 3.0.4

byte-for-byte the same sound.

Every figure comes from the plugin's own test suites and published benchmarks — the full measurements, the method and every version's history are on GitHub.

MANUAL · NEURAL DE-ESSER + WARMTH

Axell Velvet: getting started and every control

Silk on your esses. Warmth everywhere else. This page explains every control, with the plugin's own screens. Back to Axell Velvet.

Getting started

  1. Install. Windows: run the Axell Signature Suite installer (or copy Axell Velvet.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3). Linux: unpack the archive and run ./install.sh.
  2. Rescan. Let your DAW rescan its VST3 plugins; Axell Velvet appears under the vendor Axell Yobag.
  3. Insert it on the track or bus it is made for, and start from a factory preset close to your material.
  4. Listen, then adjust the few controls described below; A/B compares two settings without losing either.

What it does

The vocal plugin of the Axell suite: a de-esser that recognises sibilants instead of listening for loud treble, and three analog saturation models (tape, console, tube) to give a voice its signature warmth. The two are linked: S-GUARD keeps the warmth off the esses, so the saturation never makes them harsh again.

DE-ESS is a one-knob de-esser. AMOUNT sets how far the esses come down. There is no threshold: the same setting works on a quiet verse and a loud chorus, at any recording level.

  • Detection: a small neural network trained on 111 voices of labelled speech (many accents) recognises s, z, sh, zh, ch and j by their sound (noisiness, spectral shape, how long the noise lasts). It is not a treble meter.
  • Cut: only the frequencies where each ess is harsh are lowered. An "s" whistling at 8.7 kHz is cut at 8.7 kHz, a "sh" at 3.3 kHz at 3.3 kHz. Vowels, "t" bursts and hi-hat bleed are left alone.
  • STUDIO / LIVE:
    • STUDIO (default, for mixing): ten linear-phase bands with 4 ms look-ahead; 14 ms latency, compensated by the host.
    • LIVE (for recording while you hear yourself through the plugin): zero latency. A causal detector with its own network, and the same cut made minimum phase. With HEAT off the plugin adds no latency at all; with HEAT on, the oversampler's 1.7 ms (0.4 ms at 96 kHz).
    • In both, with nothing to cut the output is the input bit for bit.
  • More controls:
    • RANGE: the most any band may be cut.
    • FOCUS: Auto / S / SH.
    • LISTEN: hear only what is removed.
    • The display shows every ess, the target and the reduction live.

HEAT offers three models, run at 16x oversampling:

  • TAPE: magnetic tape (Jiles-Atherton hysteresis with record and playback emphasis). Treble and esses saturate first, as on tape; the low end gets a playback head bump. Smooth, glued, odd harmonics.
  • CONSOLE: an input transformer (low, loud notes saturate first) into a class-A transistor stage. Punch, weight, presence; 2nd and 3rd harmonics.
  • TUBE: a triode stage (Koren's tube equations on a real load line) with grid conduction and bias shift. Sweet, mostly 2nd harmonic, blooms on loud notes.

Each model has these controls:

  • DRIVE (0-10): calibrated so every model adds the same amount of harmonics at the same setting (-55 dB at 0, -35 dB at 5, -15 dB at 10). The level is matched automatically, so turning it up adds character, not volume.
  • S-GUARD: how much the esses are kept out of the heat.
  • TONE: dark ↔ bright.
  • MIX: parallel saturation.
  • The display shows the model's curve at your vocal's current level and the harmonics it adds.

GLOBAL:

  • LEVEL + LEARN: play the vocal and press LEARN. LEVEL is set so the models meet your recording at their nominal level, so DRIVE means the same on every recording. LEVEL never changes the output volume.
  • LOW CUT (Off / 30 / 60 / 90 Hz, 24 dB/oct): removes rumble before the models, which would otherwise distort the voice with it. The default 30 Hz takes only what you can't hear.
  • OUTPUT, oversampling 2x-16x, A/B, user presets.

There are 28 factory presets in six groups:

  • Essentials
  • Rap & Hip-Hop
  • Singing
  • Special
  • Tracking (Live): the LIVE de-esser for recording, alone or with light tape.
  • MY VOICE: six presets made on Axell's own voice and recording chain (a large-diaphragm condenser into an interface preamp with a FET compressor), one of them for tracking. They also set LEVEL and a 90 Hz low cut for that chain.

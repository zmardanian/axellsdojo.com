MANUAL · VOICE-AWARE GATE

Axell Gate: getting started and every control

A gate that hears your voice, not just its level. This page explains every control, with the plugin's own screens. Back to Axell Gate.

Getting started

  1. Install. Windows: run the Axell Signature Suite installer (or copy Axell Gate.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3). Linux: unpack the archive and run ./install.sh.
  2. Rescan. Let your DAW rescan its VST3 plugins; Axell Gate appears under the vendor Axell Yobag.
  3. Insert it on the track or bus it is made for, and start from a factory preset close to your material.
  4. Listen, then adjust the few controls described below; A/B compares two settings without losing either.

What it does

The gate of the Axell suite, built for vocals and beats:

  • VOCAL opens on your voice, not on a level. It keeps every word start, sets breaths to the level you choose, hears your room (it tells the voice from the room ringing on after it) and, with the beat on its side-chain, removes the beat's headphone bleed in the gaps and under the voice.
  • SHAPE shortens drum tails without clicks and without the kink a release puts in a decay.
  • RHYTHM chops on the host's grid with click-free edges.

VOCAL

  • SMART detection: no threshold to set; the same setting works on a whisper and a shout. Four parts:
    • Two voice-activity networks (3.0): 2.0's, trained on 27 voices with headphone bleed, room noise, rumble, untreated-room reverberation, compression and breaths, decides wherever it is sure; on doubtful frames a second network, trained on 111 voices, must agree. Far less voice lost on voices it never heard, fewer false openings.
    • Room model: learns your room's reverb time by itself, so the tail after a word is not taken for voice.
    • Pause model: knows that a 40 ms closure inside a word is not a pause.
    • Decision: a hidden Markov model over a short look-ahead.
  • SPEED: BEST (67 ms latency), FAST (42 ms), LIVE (0 ms, a classic level gate for monitoring while you record).
  • SENSITIVITY: opens more or less readily.
  • BREATH: how loud breaths stay (removed .. untouched).
  • BLEED REMOVAL: route the beat into the side-chain input. A frequency-domain Kalman filter learns how the beat leaks from your headphones into the mic and subtracts it. It never needs a threshold and never adds bleed (a safety check scales it down when the estimate does not fit). What it learned is saved with the project. The meter shows how much quieter the gaps are (GAPS), how well the learned leak fits (FIT) and the beat's level. FREEZE keeps what it learned.
  • LEVEL detection (and LIVE): KEY HP / KEY LP filter what the level detector hears; KEY LISTEN lets you hear it.

SHAPE (programmed drums, samples, 808s)

  • TAIL: decay scaling. The gain follows each hit's own envelope, g = (e / e_peak)^alpha, so an exponential tail stays exponential, just faster. TAIL 50 % = half as long, same shape, no click. The opening ramp ends exactly at the onset (look-ahead), even on a hit that lands on the previous one's ringing tail.
  • THRESHOLD, HIT SENS, ATTACK, HOLD, RELEASE (the final close), RANGE.
  • MIDI out: a note for every hit (HIT NOTE; velocity from the hit), exactly where you hear the hit. Layer a sample, or drive a synth from the drums.

RHYTHM

  • 16 steps, each with its own level (LENGTH 2-16; 12 for triplet bars), RATE 1/4 to 1/32 and triplets, SWING, EDGE (raised-cosine edges centred on the grid), RANGE.
  • It follows the host's tempo and position; stopped, it free-runs.

There are 25 factory presets (vocals, drums, rhythm), user presets and A/B. Projects and presets from 1.0 load.

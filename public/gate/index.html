01   WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Five reasons, all measured.

7.7 dB less damage to the voice

Than a classic gate tuned perfectly for every take - with the same background removal.

Takes the beat out of your vocal

Put the beat on the side-chain: the gaps get 11-15 dB cleaner, and 10-13 dB of headphone bleed goes under the voice.

Never cuts a soft syllable

PITCH HOLD keeps the zero-latency gate open for as long as the voice is still pitched.

A drum shaper with MIDI out

SHAPE shortens drum tails with 10-55 dB less click than a release - and sends one MIDI note per hit, within 3 ms.

Featherweight

Put it on every track: VOCAL mode uses 1.5 % of one core, SHAPE and RHYTHM under 0.2 %.

Every figure comes from the plugin's own test suites and published benchmarks — the full measurements, the method and every version's history are on GitHub.

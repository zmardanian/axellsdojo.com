Most gates open on level. Axell Gate opens on your voice. Two voice networks trained on 111 voices tell a word from a breath, a breath from the room, and the room from the beat bleeding out of your headphones - then remove what isn't you, in the gaps and under the voice.

01   WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Five reasons, all measured.

7.7 dB less damage to the voice

than a classic gate whose every setting was searched per take - a perfect engineer - at equal background removal.

Takes the beat out of your vocal

beat on the side-chain: gaps 11-15 dB cleaner, 10-13 dB of headphone bleed removed under the voice.

Never cuts a soft syllable

PITCH HOLD keeps the zero-latency gate open while the voice is still pitched.

A drum shaper with MIDI out

SHAPE shortens tails with 10-55 dB less click than a release, and sends one MIDI note per hit, within 3 ms.

Featherweight

VOCAL mode 1.5 % of one core; SHAPE and RHYTHM under 0.2 %.

Every figure comes from the plugin's own test suites and published benchmarks — the full measurements, the method and every version's history are on GitHub.
